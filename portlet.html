Recording Veteran Status in ESR

In order for us to provide the support you need where necessary, we would appreciate it if you take the time to record your Veterans status on the Wellbeing Form.

This also applies to Armed Forces Family Members, Armed Forces Reservists and Cadet Armed Forces Volunteers.

Click the button below to open My ESR and navigate to Personal Information -> Wellbeing tab.

Special Leave Policy

The Trust's Armed Forces policy is replaced with our Special Leave Policy (QP3636 / HR04 v4.3) while our dedicated Armed Forces Policy is being finalized.

  • Grants 10 days' paid leave per year for Reservists and Cadet Force Adult Volunteers for required training.
  • Provides support and job protection during active service mobilisation.
  • Includes terms for Emergency Domestic, Carers, Bereavement, and Civic duties leave.

Armed Forces Health Training (Module 1)

The NHS Armed Forces healthcare training and education programme is being delivered across England, with a dedicated Veterans Covenant Healthcare Alliance (VCHA) trainer for each region.

This programme will support the NHS wide system to demonstrate that the NHS and their partners receive the appropriate training and education required to ensure they can develop a skilled, educated, and inclusive workforce.

Armed Forces Staff Network

Whether you are a Veteran, Reservist, member of the Armed Forces Community, or have family/friend connections, join our Armed Forces Staff Network to chat to others in the community in a friendly, welcoming space. The group currently meets online once a month.

Please email network chair Simon Glazebrook (simon.glazebrook@nhs.net) for more information about the next meeting.
