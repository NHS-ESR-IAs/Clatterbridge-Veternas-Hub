Recording Your Veteran Status in ESR

In order for us to provide the support you need where necessary, we would appreciate it if you take the time to record your Veterans status on the Wellbeing Form.

This also applies to Armed Forces Family Members, Armed Forces Reservists, and Cadet Armed Forces Adult Volunteers.

How to update in ESR:
  1. Log in to the NHS Electronic Staff Record (ESR) portal.
  2. Navigate to your Personal Information / My ESR section.
  3. Select the Wellbeing tab.
  4. Complete the Armed Forces questions and submit your details.

To record or update your status, click the button below to log in to My ESR, then follow the 4 simple steps above:

Open My ESR Dashboard →
Why record your status?

Recording your status helps our Trust understand the size and needs of our Armed Forces community, ensures you receive relevant support, and helps us uphold our commitments under the Armed Forces Covenant.

Special Leave Policy (QP3636 / HR04 v4.3)

Note: We are currently working on dedicated standalone Armed Forces Policy documentation which will be published soon. In the meantime, all leave provisions for Reservists and Cadet Force Adult Volunteers are governed under our Trust's Special Leave Policy.

This policy applies to all employees of The Clatterbridge Cancer Centre NHS Foundation Trust. It creates a fair and consistent framework for leave, including:

  • Training for Reservists & Cadet Force Adult Volunteers (CFAVs): The Trust grants 10 days' paid leave per annual leave year for Reservists and CFAVs to attend required training commitments (Section 7.33).
  • Active Military Service / Mobilisation: Unpaid leave granted for active duty with protection of employment status (Section 7.36).
  • Emergency Domestic & Carers Leave: Support for unexpected personal emergencies and dependant care.
  • Bereavement, Civic Duties & Fertility Leave: Compassionate time off and support for public service.

NHS Armed Forces Healthcare Training & Education

The NHS Armed Forces healthcare training and education programme is being delivered across England, with a dedicated Veterans Covenant Healthcare Alliance (VCHA) trainer for each region. Our regional trainers play a vital role by working both within and beyond the NHS to improve care for the Armed Forces community (serving personnel, reservists, veterans and their families).

This programme will support the NHS wide system to demonstrate that the NHS and their partners receive the appropriate training and education required to ensure they can develop a skilled, educated, and inclusive NHS primary, community and secondary care workforce, to meet the evolving needs of the Armed Forces community within the NHS in England.
